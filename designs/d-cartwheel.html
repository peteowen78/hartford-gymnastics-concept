Three steps to your first session

  1. 1

    Email us for a place

    Pick a session and ask. We reply to confirm your place before you come.

  2. 2

    Try two sessions

    Pay as you go for the first two. Arrive five minutes early and a coach will meet you inside.

  3. 3

    Join the family

    Register with British Gymnastics before your third visit, then book monthly to keep your place.

Who grins, wins!

Our club is built on the feeling of family. Children work their way through our groups, grow up, and come back with children of their own. Whatever the goal, small and personal or large and developmental, the motto stays the same.

  • Two welfare officers
  • First-aid trained senior coaches
  • Schools and disability groups welcome
