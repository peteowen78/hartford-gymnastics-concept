Have a Go: the brand, worked up

Direction E developed into a kit of parts: logo versions, a cast of eight gymnasts, two colourways and a few things it would live on. Everything here is a first proposal for discussion. Open the homepage (Club Green) · Sunshine version

Logo

A gymnast mid star-jump beside the name. It works wide, stacked, as a round icon for social profiles, and in one colour for embroidery and print.

The cast

Eight moves, one way of drawing: a round head, four limbs in one thick line, and a leotard in any brand colour. New moves are quick to add, so the set can grow with the club (vault, bars, a coach, a wheelchair gymnast).

Two colourways

Club Green leads: the same brand on the green the club already owns, with white figures in yellow leotards. Sunshine is the secondary colourway, for moments that want extra warmth such as pre-school classes, stickers and social posts.
