Can people find Hartford School of Gymnastics online?

We looked at your website the way Google and a first-time visitor do. Here is what we found, in plain English, with no jargon.

5 of 10

Your site passes 5 of the 10 basic checks. Google can find the site and it works on a phone. Five gaps affect how the club appears once it is found, and all five are fixable.

The one that matters most

In search results, the club is called "Mysite".

Every page has a title. It is the blue link people see in Google and the name on the browser tab. Yours reads "Welcome and Class Information | Mysite".

"Mysite" looks like a placeholder left over from when the website was first set up. The title never says Hartford, gymnastics, or that this is a club for children, which are the words a parent would search for.

It is a one-line change, and probably the quickest win on this page.

Four smaller things to fix

None of these is hard. Together they decide how you look in search results and when a parent shares your link.

To fix

There is no description for Google to show

Under the blue link in a search result there are two lines of text. Your site does not supply any, so Google has to pick some words from the page itself.

A new site would say it for you: gymnastics classes for children in Hartford, with ages and how to join.

To fix

Shared links show no photograph

When a parent sends your link to another parent, no picture or description goes with it, and the title says "Mysite". Word of mouth between parents is how clubs like yours fill classes.

A new site would attach a picture, the club's name and one clear line to every share.

To fix

Most pictures have no description

Each picture can carry a short written description, used by image searches and read aloud to visitors who cannot see the screen. Of 12 pictures, 8 have none, and one is labelled "fgfg.JPG".

A new site would describe every picture in plain words.

To fix

Google is not handed the club's details

Websites can give Google a small fact sheet: what you are, where you train, and how to get in touch. Yours has none.

A new site would include that fact sheet, marked up as a sports club in Hartford.

To fix

A summary file for AI tools

Your site already has an AI summary file, made automatically by Wix. It describes the club, but its title calls the site "Mysite", the same placeholder as your page title.

Fixing the page title would most likely fix the summary file too, since both come from the same setting.

Nobody can make an AI recommend a business, and anyone who offers to is selling nothing. What a website can do is let these tools reach it, read it and get your facts right.

What we would do

The club is easy for Google to reach, but what Google then shows is a placeholder name and no description. A new site would fix all five points above and make it obvious, in the search result itself, who you are and who the classes are for.
